# IEEE Std 754-2019

## Why this source

The specification pfloat implements: the five rounding direction
attributes, the exception flags, special value semantics, and the
recommended function special cases. Behavior is anchored to clause
numbers throughout the tree; the clause to module map lives in
`docs/references.md`.

## What it grounds

Every kernel's rounding and exception contract. The load bearing
clauses cited in tree: 4.3 (rounding attributes), 5.3.1 (remainder),
5.4.1 (square root), 5.11 (comparison with NaN), 6.2/7.2/7.3 (special
values and exceptions), 6.5 (rounding funnel), 7.6 (INEXACT), 9.2,
9.2.1, 9.4, 9.4.3 (recommended functions and their special cases).

## Alternatives

None: it is the standard. The free proxy literature for readers without
IEEE access is Goldberg (`goldberg-1991.md`) and the Muller et al
Handbook (`muller-handbook-fp-2018.md`). For semantics beyond the fixed
formats (arbitrary precision), the MPFR semantics are the de facto
extension specification (entry lands with the oracle sweep).
