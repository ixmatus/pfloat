# Muller's Table Maker's Dilemma page (2000 snapshot)

## Why this source

The first public home of the Lefevre-Muller worst case results, named in
the corpus provenance document as the historical second link of the
lineage chain (paper, this page, the maintained vinc17 database, the
CORE-MATH `.wc` files). It documents what the 2001 paper's data looked
like when the field first consumed it.

## What it grounds

Nothing in the implementation; it is the historical anchor that lets a
future maintainer trace where the hard to round corpus came from when
the maintained successors move or vanish.

## Alternatives

`vinc17-testlibm.md` is the maintained successor and
`core-math-wc-corpus.md` the machine readable form pfloat actually
samples; the Muller et al Handbook is the print consolidation.
